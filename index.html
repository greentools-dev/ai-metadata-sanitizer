AI Metadata & C2PA Extraction FAQ

What is C2PA metadata?

The Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA) embeds cryptographically signed JUMBF manifests into media files recording software origin, edit history, and whether generative AI was utilized.

How do Instagram and TikTok detect AI media?

Social algorithms do not visually scan videos for artifacts; instead, automated ingest filters check for IPTC digitalSourceType tags (e.g. trainedAlgorithmicMedia) and C2PA UUID boxes. Excising these headers stops automated AI badge enforcement.

Why is binary excision superior to ffmpeg re-encoding?

Re-encoding a video takes significant processing time, degrades visual quality through lossy compression, and can introduce stutter. Binary excision directly removes metadata boxes in under 50ms with 0% quality loss.

Is my media uploaded to any server?

No. The entire DataView parsing and array excision occurs in your browser's local memory. Your files never leave your device.
